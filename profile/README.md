# Luna Rigel Labs

Independent software and AI research lab based in Peru.

We design, build, and study software systems at the intersection of artificial intelligence, human-computer interaction, digital culture, and creative technology.

## Areas of work

- Artificial intelligence and human-AI interaction
- Android and software development
- AI evaluation and experimentation
- Digital culture and social research
- Creative technology and interactive systems

## Current direction

We are developing experimental software, research tools, and digital products focused on performance, usability, responsible AI, and meaningful human interaction with technology.

## Principles

- Build useful technology
- Question assumptions
- Protect user autonomy and privacy
- Combine technical experimentation with social research
- Favor evidence over hype

Based in Peru. Building for a global audience.
